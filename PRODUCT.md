## Platform

web

## Users

Three primary audiences, of equal priority:

- **NHS and health professionals**: clinicians, practice and service managers, commissioners and
  system leaders (ICB/ICS) looking for expertise, events, training and collaboration, mostly in UK
  primary and community care.
- **Partners and funders**: industry, charities, councils, grant bodies and other organisations
  weighing whether to partner with, sponsor or fund Pathfinder's work.
- **Researchers and academics**: universities and researchers interested in innovation, evaluation
  and joint research.

Community members and patients are served by Pathfinder's mission but are not a primary audience
for this site.

## Product Purpose

Pathfinder Healthcare Developments CIC is a community interest company whose central role is to
**convene and connect**: it brings health systems, professionals, industry and communities together,
through events, conferences and partnerships, to drive change in healthcare.

Its stated mission *(current site)*: "to improve access to healthcare services for all individuals,
regardless of their socio-economic status or geographic location, and to promote health equity
worldwide." Its purpose *(current site)*: "to advocate for Universal Health Care and to advance the
importance of primary care as a critical component of any healthcare system."

The site succeeds when each audience can, with equal ease:

1. **Start a partnership**: enquire about collaborating, sponsoring, funding or co-developing work.
2. **Attend an event**: find, understand and register for Pathfinder summits and conferences.
3. **Use the research**: read or download reports and publications, and come away trusting
   Pathfinder's expertise.

No single path outranks the others.

## Positioning

Pathfinder is a convenor with a public-benefit mandate: a community interest company, rooted in UK
primary care, that turns convening (summits, events and partnerships) into published evidence
(delivery reports) and onward collaboration. A commercial events company cannot truthfully claim the
CIC mandate and primary-care leadership; an academic body or think tank does not run the convening.

## Product Principles

1. **Three doors, equal weight.** Partnership, events and research are equally important routes.
   No one of them is buried behind another.
2. **Convening is the proof.** Pathfinder's credibility comes from the rooms it convenes, the people
   in them and what they publish afterwards. Show that real evidence, not general claims of impact.
3. **Earned trust for expert readers.** Clinicians, commissioners, funders and academics read
   critically. Be precise, specific and evidence-led; avoid charity-sector sentiment and corporate
   filler.
4. **Equity in practice.** An organisation advocating universal access must be exemplary in
   accessibility, performance and plain language.
5. **Bespoke, not generic.** The site must not read as a standard corporate healthcare website.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

**WCAG 2.2 AA as the minimum, and AAA wherever possible** (owner's requirement). Includes: AAA
text contrast (7:1 for body text) where practicable, full keyboard access, screen-reader support,
respect for reduced-motion preferences, readable line lengths and plain-language copy. Downloads
(reports) should be accessible PDFs, or offered alongside an accessible web version.
